I

The money is there.

Small business grants are funding you never pay back. The hard part has never been whether the money exists. It is finding the legitimate opportunities, and then writing an application a reviewer says yes to.

Money Time helps entrepreneurs find, apply for, and win small business grants so they can secure the funding they need to grow. Every week on YouTube, Ebonie walks through real applications line by line, so you see what a strong answer looks like before you write your own.

The goal is simple: a world where every entrepreneur can access the capital they need to build a sustainable business.

Entrepreneurs shouldn't have to guess their way to funding.Money Time values
II

Walnut Park to the bar.

"I don't believe in closed doors. If I want it, I will do what is necessary to get it, even if I must build a window or door to get through."

Ebonie was born and raised in the Walnut Park neighborhood of St. Louis, and knew from a very young age that she wanted more out of life. While others were out enjoying themselves, she was head down in the books, living on ramen noodles and peanut butter sandwiches through law school.

Her view hasn't changed: laziness has nothing to do with intellect, and drive trumps intellect. If you are an entrepreneur with drive and hungry to succeed, she will help you outwork your competition.
